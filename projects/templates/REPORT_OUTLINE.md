# Report outline (answer first)

Use this for `notebooks/02_report.ipynb` or a short written report (2–6 pages). Everything quantitative should be **generated by code** from your package.

## 1. Summary (3–5 sentences, at the top)
What was the question, what did you find (with numbers and uncertainty), and what should the reader do or believe now?

## 2. Question and data
* The question in one sentence, and why it matters
* Data source, size, period, what a row represents (link to the data card)
* Population vs sample: who or what do the data represent?

## 3. Methods
* Cleaning steps and the reasons for each decision (what you removed, filled, or kept, and how many rows were affected)
* The analyses/models you used and why, how they were evaluated (train/test, cross-validation, intervals), random seeds
* Which comparisons you planned **before** looking, and how many you ran in total

## 4. Findings (one subsection per finding)
For each finding:
* **A takeaway title** ("Weekends see 11% fewer rides than weekdays")
* One chart with labelled axes and units
* The estimate **with its interval**, the effect size and the sample size
* One or two sentences of interpretation in plain language

## 5. Limitations
Specific, honest and ranked: sampling and measurement issues, confounding, where the model could fail, what you did *not* check.

## 6. Conclusion and next steps
* The answer to the question, in the strength the evidence supports (not stronger)
* 2–4 concrete next steps (more data, a better design, a follow-up question)

## Appendix
Environment report (versions), link to code, full tables, data card, ethics checklist.

### Self-check before you share
- [ ] A reader who stops after the summary still gets the answer
- [ ] Every number has a unit; every estimate has an interval
- [ ] Every chart's title states a message; axes are labelled; bars start at zero
- [ ] I separated what the data show from what I think they mean
- [ ] I did not claim causation from observational data
- [ ] The notebook runs after Restart & Run All
